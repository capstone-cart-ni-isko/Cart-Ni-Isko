1. SYSTEM SNAPSHOT
================================================================================
- Product: Cart ni Isko — web-based, mobile-accessible e-commerce + point-of-sale
  system for "Tindahan ni Isko", official merchandise of the Bicol University
  University Student Council (BU USC), Legazpi City, Albay.
- Two strictly separate portals [RULE-A]:
    * Customer portal  -> React SPA at "/" (Vite + React Router), bottom nav on
      mobile, header + side nav on desktop.
    * Employee portal  -> same SPA under "/admin/*" (AdminLayout, sidebar rail).
- Backend: Laravel 12 JSON API under "/api/*" (Sanctum bearer tokens),
  controllers: AuthAPI, CartAPI, CheckoutAPI, OrdersAPI, PosAPI, ProductsAPI,
  AppointAPI, TrackingAPI, NotifAPI, AccountsAPI, ReviewsAPI, WishlistAPI,
  ShiftAPI, SettingsAPI, ReportsAPI, AccessAPI, UploadAPI, HealthAPI,
  InputValidatorAPI.
- Database: Supabase Postgres (Tokyo PgBouncer pooler, sslmode=require) reached
  by Laravel over PDO. One Postgres RPC exists: api_auth_cust_login(phone,
  password). No Supabase client/SDK and no Supabase REST is used by the app;
  tables are additionally REVOKE'd from anon/authenticated/service_role, so all
  data access goes through the Laravel API. Tests run on sqlite :memory:.
- Caching: GET reads cached ~30s (CacheReads middleware), catalog cached
  ~5 min in the frontend, invalidated by an "api:version" bump on mutations.
- Scheduler (php artisan schedule:work): priority-notification follow-up every
  15 min, heartbeat every minute (exposed at GET /api/health/scheduler),
  academic-period roster refresh hourly.

1.2 ROLE MODEL  [RULE-A]
  - Employees: exactly one of "super admin" | "admin" | "staff"; must currently
    be a BU student; store must have >=1 super admin; >=1 employee overall.
  - Login identity: employee = BU email + password (unique BU email);
    customer = phone number + password (unique phone).
  - Only super admin may create employee accounts.
  - Customers: exactly one of "BUeño" | "guest"; a BUeño additionally provides
    BU e-mail (unique, one BUeño only), college, department and a role of
    student|faculty|staff|alumni. An employee may also be a customer, but the
    two accounts stay separate. [NOW-C: signup collects role/college/program/
    year but cust_type is written as the submitted role, defaulting to 'Student'
    (DB default 'STUDENT'; POS walk-ins get 'WALK-IN'), never 'BUeño'/'guest';
    BU-domain and BUeño/guest enforcement is not yet validated server-side
    — [DELTA D06]].
  - Customers are NEVER given employee endpoints: EnsureRole returns 403
    "Employee authentication is required."

2. BUSINESS-RULE BASELINE CARRIED INTO EVERY FLOW  [RULE-A]
================================================================================
  R01 Store has >=1 employee; may sell >=0 products; may have >=0 customers.
  R02 An employee may also be a customer, accounts separate.
  R03 A system page has >=1 tabs; customer and employee portals are separate.
  R04 Appointments: customer books >=0; type only "visit" or "pickup";
      every appointment slot is exactly 10 minutes;
      >=1 employee must be available in store on a date/period for a slot to be
      bookable; a slot holds max 5 "pickup" appointments and max 1 "visit";
      an appointment is completed by exactly one employee by scanning the
      customer's appointment QR code.
  R05 Customer: type only "BUeño" or "guest"; BUeño gives BU email + college +
      department + role (student|faculty|staff|alumni); BU email unique to one
      BUeño; login uses own phone + password; phone unique to one customer.
  R06 Delivery: a delivery belongs to only one order; customer has >=1 delivery
      address; the profile address is automatically a delivery address;
      delivery is completed by the CUSTOMER scanning the delivery QR on the
      parcel.
  R07 Employee categories/roles and access matrix as in 1.2/1.3; own BU email +
      password login; BU email unique to one employee.
  R08 Employee scheduling: >=180 minutes availability per employee per week,
      regardless of dates; in-store availability dates/periods are preset.
  R09 Orders: customer checks out >=0; order belongs to exactly 1 customer;
      >=1 product; >=1 product variation per product; >=1 piece per variation;
      type only "pre-order" or "walk-in"; a pre-order is claimed only by
      delivery or by booking a "pickup" appointment; pre-orders are paid online
      only; a payment transaction belongs to exactly 1 pre-order; list of
      products + claiming modality + online payment must ALL be complete to
      activate a pre-order; a walk-in order is facilitated by exactly 1
      employee.
  R10 Products: a category has >=1 product; product name (case-insensitive)
      unique to one product; a product has >=1 "size" variation and >=1
      "color" variation.
  R11 Page/chrome rules from the flow sections (nav ribbons, icons) as quoted.

Requirement baseline carried from (B) [SRS-B]:
  REQ-ALR-01..03, REQ-APC-01..02, REQ-AN-01..03, REQ-AB-01..04, REQ-CW-01..03,
  REQ-IM-01..03, REQ-OC-01..02, REQ-OT-01, REQ-POS-01..02, REQ-PC-01,
  REQ-SC-01..04, REQ-SS-01..03, REQ-SD-01..04, REQ-UM-01..04, REQ-EMP_LOGIN-01..10,
  REQ-EMP_HOME-01, review rules (purchase-gated, one rating + one feedback per
  product, editable while unapproved), QR rules (unique per order, reusable only
  until claimed/cancelled/returned; staff/admin/super-admin scan in store,
  customer scans parcel QR).

NOTE ON SLOT LENGTH/CAPACITY CONFLICT: (A) says every slot is exactly 10 minutes
with max 5 pickup + 1 visit; (B) said 30 minutes for "order claiming" with max 10
customers and 10 minutes/max 1 for "visit". Per the precedence rule the flows
below state (A)'s 10-minute/5+1 rule as the rule to follow, and section 9 flags
the current implementation (30/10 minutes, capacity 10/1) as [DELTA].

3. CUSTOMER SIDE — COMPLETE USER FLOWS
================================================================================

C1. CUSTOMER SIGNUP
  Entry: Welcome "/" -> "Create an account" -> /signup (7 steps, progress kept
  in localStorage "isko_signup_progress" so a reload never loses the form
  [SRS-B REQ-ALR-02]).
  Steps:
   1 role       -> choose account role from ['Student','Alumni','Faculty',
                    'Guest'] (the intended BUeño/guest split of [RULE-A R05]
                    is approximated by these four values — see 1.2 [DELTA]).
   2 details    -> first name, last name, phone (09XXXXXXXXX), university e-mail
                   (placeholder student@bicol-u.edu.ph), username, address
                   (barangay/street block; profile address becomes the default
                   delivery address [RULE-A R06]).
   3 password   -> >=8 chars + confirm + agreement to Terms of Use; inline/
                   toast errors: "Password must be at least 8 characters",
                   "Passwords do not match", "You must agree to the Terms of
                   Use" (form never refreshes/empties on error [SRS-B REQ-ALR-02/03]).
   4 otp        -> 6-digit OTP screen (UI complete; see [DELTA] in C2b).
   5 success    -> confirmation screen.
   6 college    -> academic details (college/institute, program, year, bloc) —
                   required for a BUeño [RULE-A R05] -> "Please select your
                   academic details".
   7 complete   -> POST /api/auth/cust_signup -> server validates uniqueness of
                   phone (409 "Phone already exists") and e-mail, hashes the
                   password, inserts the customer, returns a Sanctum token ->
                   toast "Registration complete! Please sign in." -> /signin.
  Post-condition: customer lands on the product catalog after first login
  [SRS-B Account Login and Registration].

C2. CUSTOMER LOGIN
  Screen: /signin — phone + password only [RULE-A R05: "own phone number and
  password to log in"].
  Flow: validate (>=10 digits) -> POST /api/auth/cust_login -> on Supabase
  Postgres the lookup+hash check runs in one round trip via RPC
  api_auth_cust_login(phone, password); PHP Hash::check fallback otherwise.
  Outcomes:
   - unknown phone -> 401 "Invalid credentials" (message shown without clearing
     either field [SRS-B REQ-ALR-02]).
   - wrong password -> same generic 401, password retained [REQ-ALR-02].
   - cust_disabled/cust_deleted set -> 403 "Account disabled" (banned account
     cannot log in [SRS-B REQ-UM-02]).
   - success -> token + profile in memory, redirect to catalog.
  Password masking: input masked; a show/hide control exists on the auth and
  settings forms [SRS-B REQ-EMP_LOGIN-01..02]. [DELTA: auto re-mask after 3
  seconds (REQ-EMP_LOGIN-03) and the "last previously logged-in" 6-digit e-mail
  OTP gate (REQ-EMP_LOGIN-09/10) are not enforced server-side.]
  [DELTA-C2b: the OTP steps in signup, /verify-otp and /forgot-password accept
  any code client-side; no verification endpoint exists yet.]

C3. PRODUCT CATALOG (home / shop)
  Chrome [RULE-A / SRS-B FLOW-CUST_HOME-01..03]: top ribbon with "Tindahan ni
  Isko" logo leftmost, search bar centermost, and rightmost horizontal array:
  "home" icon (home tab), "bag" icon (bag tab), "appointments" icon
  (appointments tab), "notification" icon (notification tab), "menu" icon
  (opens the right-hand sidebar). The menu sidebar holds, top-to-bottom:
  "profile", "dark mode", "wishlist", "orders", "settings", "logout"
  [RULE-A FLOW-CUST_HOME-03].
  [NOW-C]: the drawer renders profile, dark mode, wishlist, orders, logout —
  the "settings" entry is missing there (Settings is still reachable from the
  Profile page, the account sidebar and the /home drawer) [DELTA].
  Flow: GET /api/products/filter {status:active} -> grid of catalog cards
  showing product name, category and base price [SRS-B REQ-PC-01]; guest and
  customer see only active (not disabled/deleted) products.
  - Search: GET /api/products/search (ilike on Postgres) from the ribbon.
  - Filter: pill chips of product categories -> re-filter the grid.
  - Sort: GET /api/products/sort (name/price/qty/created/popularity).
  - Card click -> product details (C4). Catalog data is cached (~5 min TTL) and
    prefetched on app start for a 1-second UI budget.

C4. PRODUCT DETAILS
  GET /api/products/view {prod_id | prod_tag}.
  Content: photo gallery, description, category, base price, rating average and
  review breakdown, variation pickers — size and color (>=1 size and >=1 color
  per product [RULE-A R10]) — stock/pre-order availability, quantity stepper,
  size-guide modal.
  Actions:
   - Add to bag -> POST /api/cart/add (creates/extends a CART-<8> order,
     recomputes item_amount = price x qty, cust_cart counter++).
     Out-of-stock variation -> toast "Sorry, this variant is currently out of
     stock." (the rule that an unavailable item stays in the bag but cannot be
     checked out is REQ-CW-02).
   - Wishlist toggle -> POST /api/wishlist/add or /api/wishlist/remove.
   - Rate -> five-star scale; Review -> text box; both submit
     POST /api/reviews/create with the product and the customer's own order.
   - Reviews list <- GET /api/reviews/display (guests see APPROVED only).

C5. WISHLIST
  Entry: menu sidebar "wishlist" -> /wishlist (RequireAuth: signed-out users get
  the LoginPromptModal "You need to sign in to access this feature.").
  Flow: GET /api/wishlist/display {cust_id}; add (upsert, cust_wishlist++),
  remove (cust_wishlist++ reversed), quantity update, and "move to bag"
  POST /api/wishlist/to_order which upserts the item into the customer's CART-
  order without removing the wishlist row.
  Rules honored: a wished product stays even when no longer offered but cannot
  be added to the bag [SRS-B REQ-CW-01]; wishlist and bag membership are fully
  independent [REQ-CW-03].

C6. BAG (cart)
  Entry: ribbon "bag" icon -> /cart.
  Flow: GET /api/cart/display {cust_id, tag_prefix:'CART-'} -> grouped items
  with variation, quantity steppers and line amounts.
  - Modify quantity -> POST /api/orders/add (merge) / item update.
  - Remove -> DELETE /api/cart/remove (only CART- orders; toast
    'Removed "X" from bag' with an Undo action that restores it).
  - Selection: the customer may select a subset of items or all items
    [SRS-B REQ-OC-01]; the checkout button blocks an empty selection
    ("Please select at least one item to checkout.") and blocks a mixed
    pre-order + regular selection.
  - Out-of-stock items remain visible but cannot be checked out [REQ-CW-02].

C7. ORDER CHECKOUT (pre-order activation)
  Entry: Bag -> "Checkout" -> /checkout.
  1 Order overview: variations, quantities, line amounts, subtotal, total due.
  2 Modality selection [SRS-B Order Overview + RULE-A R09]:
     - PICKUP  -> requires an open "pickup"/CLAIM appointment. If none exists
       the customer is taken to the schedule calendar (AppointmentForm) to book
       a slot, then returns to checkout with the appointment details shown in
       the overview. Server check: POST /api/checkout/dispatch returns 422
       "A valid order-claiming appointment is required." when missing.
     - DELIVERY -> address step. The profile address is automatically offered
       as the default delivery address [RULE-A R06]; the customer can type a
       different one; then a delivery speed tier: priority (higher fee, arrives
       within 24h), standard (normal fee/time), saver (lower fee, slower)
       [SRS-B Modality Selection]. Current fee table [NOW-C]:
       priority = +PHP100, ETA now()+1 day (UI label "Same-day", <=24h),
       standard = +PHP50, ETA +2 days (UI label "1-2 days"),
       saver = +PHP30, ETA +5 days (UI label "3-5 days"),
       pickup fee = 0. The server derives the fee itself; the client table is
       only a preview.
     POST /api/checkout/dispatch {ord_id, dispatch_type,...} -> subtotal,
     dispatch_fee, total_due, dispatch_details.
  3 Online payment [RULE-A R09: pre-orders are paid online only; a payment
     transaction belongs to exactly one pre-order]:
     - Direct reference flow -> POST /api/checkout/payment
     - Gateway flow -> POST /api/checkout/payment/intent {gateway:'paymongo'}
       -> PayMongo PaymentIntent -> checkout_url (card, gcash, grab_pay,
       paymaya) -> webhook POST /api/checkout/payment/webhook (HMAC-SHA256
       Paymongo-Signature) marks the order PAID and notifies customer + admins.
  4 Commit (single DB transaction, locks order + products) [REQ-OC-02: a failed
     checkout restores the original bag state]:
     a recompute line totals server-side, b create payment row (pay_ref
     'PAY-<16>'), c pickup -> create pickup row, order status 'TO CLAIM';
       delivery -> create delivery ('PENDING', delivery_ref 'DEL-<8>',
       deliver_qr 'QR-DEL-<10>') + parcel, order status 'TO RECEIVE',
     d stock check -> InsufficientStockException rolls everything back if short;
       otherwise prod_qty decrements and peak/today sales counters increase,
     e cust_cart-- , cust_orders++ , ord_tag rewritten CART- -> ORD-<8>,
     f low-stock priority notifications to ADMIN + SUPER ADMIN when
       prod_qty <= low_stock_threshold (default 5) [SRS-B REQ-IM-03].
  5 Result: the order leaves the bag and becomes an active order; a unique QR
     code is generated for it [SRS-B QR-code Verification]; customer is routed
     back to the updated bag/orders view.

C8. ORDER TRACKING
  Entry: menu "orders" -> /orders (list) -> /orders/:id (detail).
  List: GET /api/cart/display|search|sort (orders and bag rows share the orders
  table; bag rows are excluded by tag prefix), status tabs, search, sort,
  copy-order-id action.
  Status vocabulary [NOW-C, matches SRS-B pipeline]:
    TO PROCESS -> TO CLAIM (pickup) | TO RECEIVE (delivery)
              -> CLAIMED | UNCLAIMED
              -> CANCEL REQUESTED -> CANCELLED
              -> RETURN REQUESTED -> RETURNED -> REFUNDED
    (+ PAID set by the payment webhook, COMPLETED shown in history).
  Detail: itemized products, amounts, modality, appointment/delivery info,
  QR code rendering, and a tracking timeline from POST /api/tracking/create
  (fetches pickup/parcel track + qr_code). Employees and customers both track
  in real time [SRS-B Order Tracking]; order status changes push a regular
  notification except TO CLAIM / TO RECEIVE (QR-driven) and, in today's code,
  also CANCEL REQUESTED / RETURN REQUESTED [REQ-OT-01 silent set + [DELTA]].
  [DELTA: real-time is polling + short cache, not push; Reverb is installed but
  no event is broadcast.]

C9. ORDER CLAIMING
  C9a Pickup (pre-order claimed by booking a pickup appointment [RULE-A R09]):
    customer arrives at the store -> any staff/admin/super-admin scans the
    customer's order QR (scanner page, C/E14) -> POST /api/tracking/scan ->
    validation: code must match, order must be TO CLAIM, and a valid open CLAIM
    appointment must exist -> order becomes CLAIMED, pickup_completed stamped,
    the appointment is closed, customer receives "[PRIORITY] Order ORD-xxxx has
    been claimed." and all employees are notified; a non-matching/invalid scan
    keeps the status, notifies all employees "[PRIORITY] Failed QR scan ..." and
    writes an emplog QR_SCAN row [SRS-B REQ-APC-03].
  C9b Delivery (RULE-A R06: completed by the CUSTOMER scanning the delivery QR
    on the parcel): customer opens the order detail -> scanner
    (html5-qrcode camera, "Position QR code within the frame") ->
    POST /api/tracking/scan -> employee scans are REJECTED on delivery orders ->
    TO RECEIVE becomes CLAIMED, parcel_completed stamped, deliver_status
    becomes DELIVERED, both sides notified.
  A QR is unique per order and cannot be reused after CLAIMED/CANCELLED/
  RETURNED/REFUNDED [SRS-B REQ-APC-01]; scanning those states returns 409 with
  a fixed message.

C10. ORDER CANCELLATION
  Customer side: while the order is in TO PROCESS, TO CLAIM or TO RECEIVE,
  press "Cancel order" -> PUT /api/orders/update with status 'CANCEL REQUESTED'
  -> toast "Cancellation requested. The store will review it shortly."
  (a customer may also set RETURN REQUESTED, but only from CLAIMED — see C11;
  any other customer status change is refused 403 "This order change requires
  staff review.").
  Guard for walk-in accounts: an order owned by the shared walk-in customer
  (phone 0000000000, SRS-B REQ-POS-01) may only request cancel/return while it
  holds an OPEN VISIT appointment (the physical store is where the request must
  happen); otherwise 403 code 'ALR_WALK_IN_NO_VISIT' and the attempt is written
  to custlog.
  Employee side: admin/super-admin approves -> 'CANCELLED' (terminal:
  ord_completed stamped) [SRS-B: only admin/super admin approve cancels].
  Notification to the customer on every status change except the QR-driven
  and request-created ones (TO CLAIM, TO RECEIVE, CANCEL REQUESTED, RETURN
  REQUESTED) [REQ-OT-01 + [DELTA]].

C11. ORDER RETURN AND REFUND
  Customer: an order already CLAIMED -> "Return request" ->
  PUT /api/orders/update 'RETURN REQUESTED' -> toast "Return requested. The
  store will review it shortly." [SRS-B: returns are requested online in the
  same two-modality process; a walk-in customer must book a visit appointment
  first — REQ-POS-02].

6. ORDER STATE MACHINE (single source of truth)
================================================================================
  CART-<8>  (bag, editable/removable, never visible to other customers)
      | checkout: modality + online payment complete, stock decremented
      v
  ORD-<8> TO PROCESS ----(staff: stock/courier ready)----> TO CLAIM (pickup)
                                              \----------> TO RECEIVE (delivery)
  TO CLAIM   --employee scans customer QR (valid CLAIM appt)--> CLAIMED
           \--no valid scan/appointment--> UNCLAIMED
           \--customer request--> CANCEL REQUESTED --> (admin) CANCELLED
           \--customer request--> RETURN REQUESTED --> (admin) RETURNED
  TO RECEIVE --customer scans parcel QR--> CLAIMED (delivery DELIVERED)
           \--employee/customer request--> CANCEL REQUESTED / RETURN REQUESTED
  CLAIMED    --> RETURN REQUESTED --> RETURNED --> REFUNDED (after money back)
                                           \----> back to CLAIMED (replacement)
  POS-<8>    walk-in order: created, paid and completed (TO CLAIM + ord_completed)
             in one employee-facilitated transaction.
  Rules: terminal states (CLAIMED/CANCELLED/RETURNED/REFUNDED) stamp
  ord_completed; every non-QR status change notifies the customer [REQ-OT-01],
  except today's silent set TO CLAIM / TO RECEIVE / CANCEL REQUESTED /
  RETURN REQUESTED (see C16);
  CANCELLED/RETURNED/REFUNDED invalidate the order QR [REQ-APC-01].

9. KNOWN DELTAS (current system vs the two documents) — for honesty/completeness
================================================================================
  D01 Slot geometry: implemented 30-min CLAIM / 10-min VISIT with capacity
      10/1; governing rule (A) requires exactly 10-minute slots with max 5
      pickup + 1 visit.
  D02 No server-enforced OTP: signup/verify/forgot-password codes are accepted
      client-side; REQ-EMP_LOGIN-09/10 (6-digit e-mail OTP when not the last
      logged-in employee) is not implemented; REQ-EMP_LOGIN-03 (auto re-mask
      after 3s) is not enforced.
  D03 Recover-password endpoint is a stub (always a generic success message);
      no mail is actually sent (MAIL_MAILER=log).
  D04 REFUNDED/CANCELLED/RETURNED do not move money or restore stock;
      PayMongoService::refundPayment is unused; PayMongo keys are empty in
      .env and the webhook route sits inside the sanctum group.
  D05 PRODVAR / PRODSALES of the new schema are not separate tables yet
      (product JSON columns + per-product counters stand in for them), and the
      bag/wishlist counters live in CUSTOMER as specified but bag rows live in
      orders with a CART- prefix.
  D06 BU-eño/guest categorization, BU e-mail domain and BU-email uniqueness are
      not validated server-side at signup; cust_type currently stores
      'Student'/'WALK-IN'.
  D07 Staff visibility rule REQ-EMP_HOME-01 (hide Dashboard/Walk-in/Reviews/
      Sales from staff) is only partially applied (only Register and Settings
      are staff-hidden); staff can still open most screens read-only.
  D08 Customer addresses are stored per-device (localStorage) and are not yet
      synced as customer rows, though checkout uses the profile address path.
  D09 Delivery address book is not persisted server-side; delivery uses profile
      or typed address.
  D10 Notification preferences screen is informational only; follow-up job uses
      a 24h unread threshold (comments say 2h).
  D11 AdminReports screen is unrouted; some inventory filters, POS buttons
      (Hold/Discount/Recent Sales), delivery Retry/Contact, review Reply/
      Escalate and settings sections are UI stubs.
  D12 Real-time updates are polling/caching (Reverb installed but unused).
  D13 Walk-in POS update endpoint lacks transition/ownership validation.
  D14 ReportBuilder references a non-existent appoint_deleted column and an
      undefined dutyShifts relation (appointments/staffing reports fragile).
  D15 Employee top ribbon lacks the required "home", "QR", "dark mode" and
      "settings" icons (FLOW-EMP_HOME-04/07/09-13) and the employee portal has
      no theme toggle; the scanner lives only inside the Pickup screen.
  D16 The customer menu drawer omits the required "settings" entry
      (FLOW-CUST_HOME-03); Settings is still reachable from Profile, the
      account sidebar and the /home drawer.
  D17 Booking/rescheduling an appointment sends no notification, and
      notification rows carry no link column - only closed/unavailable
      appointment notices deep-link (to /appointments); REQ-OT-01's silent set
      is wider in code (also CANCEL REQUESTED / RETURN REQUESTED).
  D18 No employee notification inbox screen exists (empnotif rows are written
      but never rendered/read; the top-bar bell shows a client-side alert feed
      instead) and cust_unread/emp_unread counters are never maintained.
  D19 The left rail has no "Logout" entry/confirmation (FLOW-EMP_HOME-05/22);
      logout is only in the top-bar profile menu.
